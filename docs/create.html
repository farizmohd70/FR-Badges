Make a badge

Design a badge, test its pattern, and share it. Everything here is sized for Nuvio: the badge is 16dp tall and the width is limited.

Pattern

Nuvio shows the badge when this pattern matches a stream title. (?i) ignores capital letters.

Look

90 matches the rounded corners of Nuvio's own badges (6dp). 120 is a full pill.

Share

Shared badges are public and anonymous, and appear on the Community made page. Please keep them friendly and do not copy other people's logos.
