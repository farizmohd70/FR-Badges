How this works

  1. Pick a look for your badges.
  2. Tap the badges you want. A green tick means it is in your pack.
  3. Put them in the order you want them shown.
  4. Copy the link and paste it into Nuvio.

Nothing breaks if you press the wrong thing. Undo is at the top.
